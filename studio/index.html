Connection checklist
  1. Connect the computer output to the deck’s LINE IN.
  2. Select an output and play the calibration tone.
  3. Set the deck level below clipping; disable Dolby and noise reduction.
  4. Press REC on the deck, then start the countdown.

Keep left and right channels separate. Match input and output sample rates in your audio settings and avoid mixing direct monitoring into playback. With a Fast Track Pro, set Mix to PB while recording.

After recording

Rewind the tape and capture both channels in Decode. Keep the original files until recovery is verified.
